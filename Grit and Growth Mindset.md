# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1.Grit

**Q1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**
Grit means staying committed to a certain goal even when things get difficult during the process.
One can achieve success by staying consistent, being patient, and not giving up.


## 2.Introduction to Growth Mindset.

**Q2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**
A growth mindset means believing that our abilities can improve through learning, practice and effort. 
Instead of seeing mistakes as failures, we can use them as opportunities to learn.


## 3.Understanding Internal Locus of Control

**Q3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**
It means believing that our actions, decisions and effort have a major impact on the results we get.
Which means that instead of thinking about bad situations, one should focus on the things that are in our control.

Key points:
- Beliving in one's own ablility to figure out things
- Question our assumptions.
- Create own curriculum.
- Honor the struggle.

## 4.How to build a Growth Mindset

**Q4. What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).**
- One should believe that skills are developed while learning.
- Accepting challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes and failures.
- Put effort into improving.
- Take feedback positively.
- Learn from people who are better than you.
- Keep trying when things become difficult.

## 5. Mindset - A MountBlue Warrior Reference Manual

**Q5.What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

My ideas to take action and build growth mindset:
- Practice something regularly instead of expecting to become perfect immediately.
- Treat mistakes as a part of learning.
- Take feedback from mentors and teammates seriously.
- Focus on my own progress instead of comparing myself with others.
- Learn from people who are more experienced.
- Keep trying even when I get stuck.
