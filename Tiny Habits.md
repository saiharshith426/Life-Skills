## 2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message

### Q2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

B = MAP means **Behavior = Motivation + Ability + Prompt**.

- **M - Motivation:** The reason or desire to do something.
- **A - Ability:** How easy or difficult it is to perform the behavior.
- **P - Prompt:** Something that reminds or triggers us to perform the behavior.

We can make a new habit easier by making the action simple, having a clear reason for doing it, and connecting it with a proper prompt.

For example, if I want to practice coding every day, I can keep my laptop ready and start practicing after dinner. Here, dinner can act as the prompt and starting with one problem makes the habit easier.

### Q3. Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Shining means celebrating immediately after completing a habit. It creates a positive feeling and helps our brain connect the behavior with something good.

The celebration does not have to be big. Even feeling happy or saying "Good job" to ourselves can help. When we celebrate a behavior, we are more likely to repeat it and make it a habit.

## 4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits

### Q5. What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book explains that instead of only focusing on what we want to achieve, we should focus on the person we want to become.

For example, instead of thinking "I want to solve more coding problems", I can think "I am a person who practices coding regularly." Our habits help build our identity over time.

### Q6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

A habit can be made easier by reducing the effort required to perform it.

- Make the habit simple and small.
- Keep the things required for the habit ready.
- Remove unnecessary steps.
- Make the environment suitable for the habit.
- Start with a very small version of the habit.

For example, if I want to study every day, I can keep my laptop and study material ready so that I can start without spending time preparing.

### Q7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

A bad habit can be made harder by increasing the effort required to perform it.

- Remove the triggers that lead to the habit.
- Keep distracting things away.
- Turn off unnecessary notifications.
- Log out of distracting applications.
- Add extra steps before doing the unwanted habit.

For example, if I want to reduce social media usage, I can log out of the applications and remove them from my home screen. This makes it harder to access them whenever I feel like using them.

## 5. Reflection

### Q8. Pick one habit that you would like to do more of?

One habit I would like to do more of is **coding practice**.

I can make the cue obvious by keeping my laptop and coding environment ready. I can connect the habit with an existing routine, such as practicing after dinner.

I can make it attractive by choosing problems that are interesting to me. I can make it easy by starting with one problem instead of planning to study for many hours.

After completing the problem, I can celebrate the progress and keep track of my practice. This can make the habit satisfying and motivate me to continue.

### Q9. Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of?

One habit I would like to reduce is **unnecessary phone and social media usage**.

I can make the cue invisible by keeping my phone away while studying and turning off unnecessary notifications.

I can make the process harder by logging out of social media applications and removing them from my home screen.

I can also track the amount of time I spend on social media. Seeing how much time I am spending on it can make me more aware of the habit and help me reduce it.
